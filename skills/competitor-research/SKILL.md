# Competitor research

**Drafts only. Never post, send, or publish. The user approves anything external.** Output is one local markdown file; no webhooks, no platform APIs.

## Steps
1. Confirm the competitor, which of the user's businesses or products it is compared to, and how far back to look (default 90 days). If the user has a brand voice or positioning file, ask for its path and read it.
2. Research public pages only (their site, public listings, news). Read pages for real; no logins, no contact forms, no outreach.
3. Cite every fact with a URL and date. Mark guesses as guesses. No invented numbers.
4. Write ./competitor-<slug>.md with:
   - Bottom line: 2-3 sentences.
   - Overview: what they do, who for, where.
   - Recent moves: dated bullets, newest first.
   - Messaging and positioning: what they promise, tone, gaps against the user's voice.
   - Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats (short table).
   - Recommendations: 3-5 one-line actions, each tied to a finding.
   - Sources.
5. Recommendations are suggestions only. Do not act on them.
